Privacy Policy

App Name: Country Capital Flag
Last Updated: December 2025

1. Introduction

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how "Country Capital Flag" collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve the game experience:

  • Personal Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your Name and Email Address. This is used to create your game account and identify you on the global leaderboards.
  • Usage Data: We collect game-related data such as high scores, XP, achievements, and player level to sync your progress across devices via Google Firebase.
  • Device Information: We may collect non-personal data such as device model and OS version for technical troubleshooting.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses industry-standard services provided by Google:

  • Google Play Services / Firebase: For user authentication, cloud database, and analytics.
  • Google AdMob: To display advertisements. AdMob may use advertising identifiers to serve personalized ads. You can opt-out of personalized ads in your Android device settings.

4. Data Security and Encryption

Your data is handled with the highest security standards:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • User data is stored on encrypted servers managed by Google Firebase.

5. Data Deletion Policy (Account Deletion)

We respect your right to be forgotten. Users can request the deletion of their account and all associated data (email, name, scores, and progress) at any time.

  • In-App: Go to Settings > Delete Account. This will instantly remove your data from our servers.
  • How to request deletion via email: Send an email to compass294@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account".
  • Process: Upon receiving your request, we will delete all your personal information and game records from our Firebase database within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us for immediate removal.
