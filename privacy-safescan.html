Privacy Policy

App Name: SafeScan: QR & Barcode Security
Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how "SafeScan" collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the scanning and security features:

  • Camera Data: Our app requires access to your device's camera to scan QR codes and barcodes. We do not store or transmit your camera feed. All image processing for scanning is done in real-time on your device using Google ML Kit.
  • Scanned Content: When you scan a QR code containing a URL, the URL is processed to check for potential security threats (Safe Browsing).
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous data regarding app performance and feature usage (e.g., successful scans, flashlight usage, settings preferences) via Google Firebase Analytics to help us improve the app.
  • Device Information: We may collect non-personal data such as device model, OS version, and crash logs for technical troubleshooting.

3. No Personal Accounts

Unlike other applications, SafeScan does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or social media credentials.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses industry-standard services provided by Google to function:

  • Google AdMob: To display advertisements. AdMob may use advertising identifiers to serve personalized ads based on your interests. You can opt-out of personalized ads in your Android device settings.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): To analyze app usage trends and stability.
  • Google Safe Browsing / Security APIs: Used to verify if a scanned link is safe or malicious.

5. Data Storage and Control

You have full control over your data:

  • Local Storage: Your history of scanned codes is stored locally on your device using encrypted internal storage. This data is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Data Control: You can clear your entire scan history at any time by going to Settings > Clear History within the app.

6. Data Security

Your data is handled with high security standards:

  • All communications with security APIs and analytics services are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
